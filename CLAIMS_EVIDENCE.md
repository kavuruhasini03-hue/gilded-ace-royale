# Claims & Evidence appendix

Each claim in the write-up and dashboard, traced to its source, its result, the alternative explanation we tested, and its limitation. Numbers come from the released parquet files (`0.9.0-draft`). The local reproduction is `site/build_data.py` (DuckDB), and the full pipeline is `databricks/` (bronze → silver → gold).

| # | Claim | Table / query | Result | Alternative tested | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | The GM's bottom ten is mostly a Back Forty and lineup effect, not ten broken machines. | `prepared_daily_view` (active floor, `location_id IS NOT NULL`), ranked by `SUM(house_win_usd)`. 4-L bridge in `gold_machine_verdict` / `site/build_data.py`. | 9 of 10 are in the Back Forty. 3 have partial exposure (M-5076 49 days, M-1120 132, M-1118 55, of 181). Location and lineup are the largest components; the machine-specific residual is small. | Individual machines underperforming: the residual vs. zone-adjusted config peers is small for 9 of 10. | Zone and config references are medians. Placement isn't random, so location and lineup aren't fully separable. |
| C2 | The Back Forty walkway closure cut its demand. | `floor_walkways` (W-ENT-B40 closed Mar 3 – Apr 7, 2025). Coin-in per installed machine-day by zone and month. DiD in `gold_competing_explanations`. | March Back Forty −23.6% vs. January; other zones +7.2%. | A floor-wide seasonal dip: the other zones rose. Pre-trend regression on the daily Back Forty minus control gap. | Descriptive. Coincident events and spillover to control zones are possible. |
| C3 | M-1158 paid out far more than its game math expects. | `machine_day` × `game_math`: luck = house win − theo; σ² = Σ `sum_bet_squared` × `payout_multiplier_variance`. | Luck −$36,165; z = −4.57; hold 3.04% vs. 10.00% theo; two-sided p = 4.9×10⁻⁶; Bonferroni across 310 machines p = 0.0015. | Chance, given how many machines we screened: the result survives Bonferroni. | A lead to inspect, never an accusation. The p-value is not the probability the finding is luck. |
| C4 | Ridgeline machines fault far more often, and overnight faults wait longest. | `operations` fault tickets by manufacturer and gaming hour. | Faults per machine: Ridgeline 7.23, Northwind 3.02, Halcyon 1.93. Mean wait overnight 2.26 h vs. 0.66–1.07 h at other times. | Exposure differences: normalized per machine, and nearly all machines were present for the full window. | Ticket counts aren't dollar losses. Downtime is valued at expected theo, not recoverable cash. |
| C5 | Comp reinvestment rose after a redemption-rate change. | `player_period` comp cost ÷ tracked theo. `loyalty_ledger` points per face dollar by week. | 1.11% (Jan) → 1.32% (Jun). Points per face dollar fell from ~12.4 to ~5.8 around May 1. | Denominator choice: floor theo shows the same rise (0.73% → 0.88%). Uncarded meters as a natural control. | No untreated tracked cohort, so this is not a causal estimate of comp lift. |
| C6 | A weekday + event forecast beats a constant baseline on unseen data. | `gold_forecast_day`, `gold_forecast_holdout_metrics` (fit Jan–Apr, frozen, scored May–Jun). Reproduced in `site/build_data.py`. | MAE $218,258 → $55,271; MAPE 16.4% → 4.1% over 61 holdout days. Event effect +$135,756/day (ridge λ = 1). | The constant Jan–Apr daily mean (the required simple baseline). | Only 61 holdout days. Weather and visitation deliberately excluded to prevent leakage; events count only if announced before the day. |
| C7 | The forecast changes a decision: no technician shift in July. | `gold_forecast_tech_timing`, `gold_tech_shift_economics`, `action_catalog` (A-TECH-SHIFT $11,520). | Recoverable downtime value per shift, priced at the forecast's hourly rates, doesn't cover a 30-day package for grave or Saturday coverage. | Recovering 25% / 50% / 75% of the overlap, with full elimination as a ceiling. | Wait-time reduction is assumed, not observed. |
| C8 | Keeping $98,800 beats every other July spend. | `gold_action_plan`, `gold_action_voi`, `action_catalog`. | Only the $1,200 inspection clears the bar. Replacement, bank-move and tech-shift packages don't pay back in July. | Replacing the bottom ten (lease $2,500 + $55/day each, 14-day lead): negative July net, and the machine-effect intervals don't support it. | Scenario probabilities (e.g., 15% / 40% / 65% actionable inspection finding) are planning assumptions, not measured frequencies. |
| C9 | The comp pilot is underpowered. | Historical backtest in `gold_action_voi` (CUPED with pre-period theo). | 20,577 June-active players. 60-day MDE $7.61 (5.77%), 30-day $4.15 (6.44%); funding bar 2.5%. | A 30-day vs. 60-day horizon. | Planning SD comes from history; the true effect size is unknown. |
