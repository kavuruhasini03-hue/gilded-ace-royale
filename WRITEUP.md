# The Gilded Ace: luck, location, or a leak?

**Dashboard:** https://gilded-ace-royale-lime.vercel.app · **Code:** https://github.com/kavuruhasini03-hue/gilded-ace-royale

**Recommendation:** Replace none of the GM's bottom ten in July. Inspect M-1158 ($1,200), defer the underpowered comp pilot, and keep $98,800 in reserve.

**What the bottom ten really are.** We split every machine's result into luck, location, lineup and leak, comparing on available hours rather than raw totals. Nine of the bottom ten sit in the Back Forty, and three were on the floor for only 49–132 of 181 days. Their gap comes mostly from location and from low-earning game configs (95% RTP, low volatility). The machine-specific residual is small, so replacing the cabinets would not fix the cause.

**Competing explanation tested.** The Back Forty entrance walkway closed for renovation from March 3 to April 7. In March, Back Forty coin-in per machine-day fell 23.6% vs. January while other zones rose 7.2%, which rules out a floor-wide seasonal dip. A difference-in-differences with a pre-trend check supports a location effect, though the comparison is descriptive.

**One real lead.** M-1158 paid out $36,165 more than its game math expects (luck z = −4.57, hold 3.04% vs. 10.00% theo). The result stays significant after a Bonferroni adjustment across 310 machines (p = 0.0015). We fund one $1,200 paytable inspection. This is a lead, not an accusation.

**Model.** We forecast daily floor coin-in from weekday averages plus ridge-penalized flags for announced events and holidays. The model was fit on January–April, frozen, and scored on 61 unseen May–June days. MAE fell from $218,258 (constant-mean baseline) to $55,271, and MAPE from 16.4% to 4.1%. Pricing technician shifts with these hourly values showed that no $11,520 shift package pays back in July, so we don't fund one.

**Comps.** Comp cost per tracked theo rose from 1.11% to 1.32% after the redemption rate changed around May 1. A pilot would need a 5.77% minimum detectable effect at 60 days, above our 2.5% bar, so we defer it. Instead we propose a monitor that triggers a review if the ratio exceeds 1.50% for two straight weeks.

**Measuring success.** If the inspection finds and fixes a fault, M-1158's August luck z should return to within ±2. In August we re-run the replacement screen with the new data. The Claims & Evidence appendix traces every claim to its table, alternative and limitation.
